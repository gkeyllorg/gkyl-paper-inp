"Line-tied flux rope relaxation and reconnection: A 3D kinetic case study" (Physics of Plasmas, 2026)
Joshua Pawlak, James Juno, and Jason TenBarge

Input files used in the paper for the high and low current two-rope merging simulations and the one-rope kink instability simulation in PKPM

* **pkpm_3D_128x140_merge_caseI.lua**: two-rope merging, I0 = -750 A, 128x128x140, domain x,y = [-0.4, 0.4] m and z = [0, 10] m
* **pkpm_3D_128x140_merge_caseII.lua**: two-rope merging, I0 = -2000 A, 128x128x140, domain x,y = [-0.4, 0.4] m and z = [0, 10] m
* **pkpm_3D_96x140_kink_caseII.lua**: one-rope kink-instability, I0 = -1000 A, 96x96x140, domain x,y = [-0.3, 0.3] m and z = [0, 10] m
